Our bespoke Product Development process delivers reliable and sustainable products. From ideation to prototyping to testing through launch, we have the expertise to bring products to market successfully.

Here are a few of the long-term products that Seventh Compass has developed and delivered. Every one is Ruby on Rails.

  • txtsignal: Flagship short-code (A2P) SMS platform; built in 2004; fully CTIA compliant; 10,000+ clients served; & 1B messages sent. (US SC: 94033)
  • ReclaimTime.io: An SMS-first billable time capture layer for independent consultants and fractional executives; launched 2026; fully CTIA compliant. (US SC: 43506)
  • Group Publishing's SimplyText: A white-labeled short-code (A2P) SMS Platform designed for Group Publishing to serve church and youth ministries; built in 2008 (US SC: 40650).
  • VBS PRO: An event planning, tracking, and execution platform for summer education lessons built on contract and released in 2013.
  • First Place Racing: An event management system for cycling events, released in 2010 and served over 1,600 events.
  • FAWN Freeze Text: A custom text message alert system developed for the University of Florida to alert crop growers on time-sensitive watering schedules during freeze-related events, released in 2011.
  • LIVE Curriculum: A content and learning management system built on contract and released in 2009 for a leading digital publishing house.
